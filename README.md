# Data Analyst

I look for the decisions hidden inside business data.

My approach starts before the analysis. I first understand the business context, clarify the decision that needs to be made, and translate it into questions the data can help answer.

From there, I investigate patterns, challenge assumptions, test hypotheses, and communicate what the evidence means for the business.

I believe the value of analytics is not in producing more dashboards. It is in reducing uncertainty and helping people make better decisions with confidence.

## How I Think

**Understand the business.**

What is the business trying to achieve, and what decision needs to be made?

**Ask better questions.**

What do we actually need to understand before deciding what to do?

**Investigate the evidence.**

What patterns, relationships, changes, and exceptions does the data reveal?

**Challenge assumptions.**

Does the evidence support what we initially believe, challenge it, or leave the question unanswered?

**Translate evidence into insight.**

What does the evidence actually mean for the business, and what decision does it inform?

**Support action and measurement.**

What should be considered next, and how would we know whether the decision or intervention worked?

**Communicate with clarity.**

Can the finding, its significance, and its implications be understood by the people making the decision?

## What I Bring

A scientific background has shaped how I approach evidence: I question assumptions, investigate carefully, and avoid conclusions that the data cannot support.

My transition into analytics has built on that foundation by adding business thinking, data analysis, visualization, and decision focused communication.

## Selected Projects

### Savora

**Customer Journey Analytics & Conversion Strategy**

Savora is a savings and investment platform where customer growth was increasing, but investment growth was not keeping pace. I investigated the customer journey from sign up through investment to determine where the conversion challenge was occurring, rather than assuming where the problem began.

The analysis identified funding to investment as the largest conversion gap, uncovered evidence of investment intent among funded non investors, and identified a potential first 30 day intervention window. I also tested assumptions about customer behaviour and investigated where investment attempts were being abandoned. Recommendations focused on improving investment completion and testing targeted engagement.

### [Savora](https://github.com/oladipupoblessing39/savora-customer-journey-analytics)

### Avera

**Business Growth & Resource Prioritization Analysis**

Avera is a savings and investment business experiencing substantial growth across customers, activity, financial balances, and Revenue. With limited resources available for the next 12 months, the analysis focused on understanding where growth was coming from, whether it was creating financial value, and where additional strategic attention should be considered.

The analysis found that investment products accounted for the majority of financial growth, while a group of customer segments contributed the largest absolute Revenue increases. Opportunity assessment combined Revenue growth, Total Balance growth, and recent momentum to identify where further strategic attention was most strongly supported by the evidence.
